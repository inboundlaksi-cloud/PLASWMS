PLAS WMS v5.13.9 — QUOTA + AX ON HAND SAFE
Build date: 2026-08-20

F-ZONE / FIRESTORE QUOTA
- Academy no longer subscribes to up to 5,000 events on every PLAS startup.
- Academy listeners start only when the Academy/profile screen is opened.
- Event history is bounded to the latest 500 events; redemptions to 200.
- Automatic Academy backfill at startup was removed. Manual sync runs once and asks for confirmation.
- Daily soft-cap counters are stored on the employee profile, so scoring no longer requires a large event download.
- F-Zone fallback now distinguishes database quota, network failure, and bridge service failure.

AX ON HAND POLICY
- On Hand comes from AX `Qty Onhand` only.
- Blank/missing `Qty Onhand` becomes 0.
- `InventQty` and `Quantity` are retained as reference/incoming data and never used as On Hand.
- Active AX_COPY records created by older versions are repaired automatically in batches after items load.
- AX cards and detail dialogs show `On Hand: 0` explicitly.
